Data Deletion

You can request the deletion of your personal data from Insaf AI at any time. Here's how.

1 What Data We Store

When you use Insaf AI, we store the following types of data:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and password (hashed)
  • Facebook/Social Page Data: Page ID, Page Name, and Page Access Token
  • Message Data: Customer messages received and AI responses sent through your connected pages
  • Product Data: Products you add to your catalog (names, images, prices, etc.)
  • Order Data: Customer orders processed through the AI agent
  • AI Settings: System prompts, bot configurations, and preferences

2 How to Request Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data using any of these methods:

1

Disconnect Page

Go to your Dashboard → Facebook Connection → Click "Disconnect" to remove all page data.

2

Delete Account

Contact us via email to request complete account and data deletion.

3

Email Request

Send an email to support@insafboost.com with your registered email and deletion request.

3 What Happens After Deletion

Once your deletion request is processed:

  • Your account and all personal data will be permanently removed
  • All connected Facebook/social media page data will be disconnected and erased
  • All stored messages and conversation history will be deleted
  • All product catalog data and order history will be removed
  • AI configuration, system prompts, and bot settings will be erased

⚠️ This action is irreversible. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

4 Processing Time

Data deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

In some cases, we may retain certain data for a limited period if required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention). Any retained data will be securely stored and not used for any other purpose.

Request Data Deletion

Send us an email with your registered email address and we'll process your request promptly.
